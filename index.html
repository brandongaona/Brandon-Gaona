About Brandon

I'm a double major in Computer Science and Mathematics at Stevens Institute of Technology in Hoboken, graduating in May 2028. I like problems where the math matters as much as the code: classification models that people make decisions from, trading strategies that need honest backtests, and low-level systems where every bit is accounted for.

This past summer I was the sole developer on a machine learning pipeline at the FCC's Office of Economics & Analytics, which sorted more than 373,000 consumer complaints and flagged tens of thousands of duplicates. Before that I did quantitative research on ETF strategies at Stevens' Hanlon Financial Systems Center and built production websites at GDF Capital.

I'm looking for Summer 2027 internships in applied AI and machine learning, especially in fields where accuracy is non-negotiable. I'm also fluent in Spanish.
