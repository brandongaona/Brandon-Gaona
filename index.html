Consumer Complaint Intelligence Pipeline

Federal Communications Commission, Summer 2026 · Python, spaCy, scikit-learn, FAISS, Flask

A 5-layer, 15-stage pipeline I designed and built alone to make sense of hundreds of thousands of free-text complaints filed with the FCC. It embeds each complaint, reduces dimensions with PCA, clusters them into 25 categories, and feeds an interactive dashboard that economists used to explore the data.

A supervised classifier assigns new complaints to categories, and a fraud-detection layer catches duplicate and coordinated submissions. Recalibrating the anomaly signals took false data-quality flags from 79% down to 7–8%.
